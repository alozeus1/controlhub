# ControlHub Manager Demo

This demo script is designed for engineering leads, team managers, and HR partners to easily understand how to navigate the new unified Workforce Command Center inside ControlHub.

## Prerequisites
- Logging in natively using HR privileges (`hr_admin` or `admin` role).

## Step 1: Navigating to the Workforce Dashboard
1. On your sidebar, click **People**.
2. Notice the `Workforce Command Center` displaying overarching KPIs at the top:
   - **Total Roster:** Highlights total workforce headcount.
   - **Composition:** Visual drill-down of Interns vs. Full-time employees via `Recharts`.
   - **Active Cohorts:** Direct links into running tracks.
3. Observe the newly integrated **Contract Management Form**. Click `Add Talent` to view the comprehensive payment scheduling form, capable of allocating individuals to fixed stipends, salary, and tracking offer letter signatures.

## Step 2: Running the Academy Hub
1. Navigate to the **Internship Program** view.
2. The UI is now broken down sensibly into 4 clear Tabs mapping the entire lifecycle:
   - **Overview:** Program level administration.
   - **Cohorts & Members:** Group your workforce logically.
   - **Requirements:** Global templates dictating checklist items required for graduation.
   - **Analytics:** Provides clear retention rate metrics alongside salary compliance percentages.

## Step 3: Utilizing the AI Copilot for Workforce Exporting
1. Hover over the **Agent/Copilot** interface in the platform.
2. Submit a natural language request: _"Export a breakdown of the Q3 Software Engineering Cohort including graduation status."_
3. Wait just 3 seconds for the Agent to process the request against `InternshipProgram` schemas.
4. Download the generated `.csv` directly from the chat interface. No manual SQL required.

_ControlHub provides a unified pane of glass over both hardware assets and the humans driving them. Enjoy the platform!_
